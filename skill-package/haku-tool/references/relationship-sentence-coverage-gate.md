# Relationship Sentence Coverage Gate

Use this gate only for a Haku-style relationship, friendship, or memory edit with two or more people. It is a source-selection and construction gate, not an effect recipe.

## Evidence scope

This working rule is grounded in direct frame/contact-sheet review of two public-reference-only local Haku Reels:

- `DS5xPk1E_NT`: tactile hand/water and light details, human witnesses, scale releases, and a legible two-person toast are interleaved as one emotional field.
- `DVdX-WBk4L5`: a pair's shared bubble action is split into individual sensory details, movement through the same classroom, and a changed final paired camera tableau.

It is additionally tested once, but not user-accepted, in `lupine-relationship-sentence-v001`: hand through lupines -> gaze turning to smile -> shared walk -> embrace. It may guide a new proof, but it is not a promoted universal Operator.

## Required source roles

The source audit must identify all four roles as actual moments, with source time ranges and hashes:

1. **Shared material action** — two people interact through a prop, body action, or mutually visible event. A posed two-shot alone does not qualify.
2. **Individual sensory witness** — one person's touch, face, gaze, or small action reveals what the shared event means. It must be a real response or action, not an interchangeable portrait.
3. **Spatial return or shared travel** — the same world is reintroduced through movement, a framing change, or a clear location consequence. A generic colour-matched landscape does not qualify.
4. **Changed paired end state** — the pair appear together again in a state that is meaningfully different from the opening: for example, play becomes joint looking, distance becomes proximity, or travel becomes a held relation.

## Visual coverage gate (separate from story coverage)

Passing the four relationship roles does **not** automatically mean the material can carry a finished Haku-style edit. Before rendering, require at least two genuinely different, native frame families among the chosen moments:

- a tactile/object/action detail;
- an individual reaction or face passage;
- a two-person interaction frame;
- a spatial-release or destination frame.

They must be real complementary coverage, not digital crops from the same locked master, duplicated time ranges, or a static wide shot relabelled as a close-up. A locked single-master source may still be used for a narrow action-spine proof, but its relationship edit must be recorded as `partial_pass` when selection and timing alone do not create a perceptibly stronger visual phrase than the plain version.

This distinction is directly supported by the `flower-exchange-relationship-sentence-v001` partial pass (complete causal relationship roles but only one fixed frame family) and the rechecked `picnic-sensory-scale-v003` branch (native tactile detail, action owner, individual reaction, and group relationship coverage).

## Edit construction

- Start with the most tactile or humanly legible entry, not with a generic wide shot.
- Isolate only one or two individual detail passages, then return to the relation before the emotional thread disappears.
- Make every cut name its continuity carrier: shared people, prop/action, location/light, movement direction, or real emotional response.
- Treat wide shots as consequences of a visible action, not atmosphere filler.
- End in a native paired state. Do not manufacture a climax with a digital zoom, repeated frame, synthetic PIP, or an unrelated stock portrait.
- Sparse black or title breaths are permitted only if they separate an already complete phrase; they cannot repair missing action coverage.

## Reject or down-route

Reject the relationship route, or down-route it to `minimal-single-idea`, when any required role is missing. In particular reject a montage that has only the same palette, only posed smiling people, unrelated locations, a reaction with no cause, or a final static frame used to pad runtime.

## Review standard

Verify each source role in the raw audit before rendering. Then directly inspect original, plain, proof, every cut boundary, and the end state. Record `pass`, `partial_pass`, or `fail` separately from the user's verdict. Only three genuinely different **user-accepted** situations may promote a tested Operator.
