## Plain versus proof

The plain sequence is calmer and immediately readable: tactile detail, pour, reaction, and group play one after another. The proof contributes real in-frame authorship. The pour causes an individual pleasure image, that image yields to the group, and the small group state becomes the full relationship field.

The proof is therefore more explicitly Haku-derived, but it is not an unconditional improvement. The portrait and group windows remain visibly designed objects. A viewer may prefer the plain edit's ease, and trustworthy full-speed playback capture is unavailable. This is why the research verdict is `partial_pass` rather than `pass`.
