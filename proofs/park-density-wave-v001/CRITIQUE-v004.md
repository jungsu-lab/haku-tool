## Plain versus proof

The plain edit is cleaner and easier to parse because the relationship states play full frame one after another. The proof edit contributes a Haku-like density wave: future relationship states coexist briefly, the lift inset anticipates the physical climax, and that inset becomes the full-frame action.

The proof therefore adds real edit structure, but it is not an unconditional visual improvement. The two live windows compete with the central pair and can still be read as a visible collage choice. This is why the research verdict is `partial_pass`, not `pass`.
