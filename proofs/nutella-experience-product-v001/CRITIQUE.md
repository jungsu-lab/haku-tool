## What passes

The fixed 9:16 crop preserves the complete lid interaction, open jar, nose response, face, and readable Nutella label. The proof establishes a real progression: hand contacts and turns the lid, the lid releases, the person brings the open jar to her nose and closes her eyes, and a later same-session shot returns to a steady front-facing product.

The plain comparison opens on the later closed-jar hold and then resets to the earlier opening action. The proof removes that causal reset. No zoom, freeze, retime, reverse, animated crop, transition effect, synthetic packshot, or padding is used.

## Why this is not a full pass

The product name is readable from the first proof frame, so the material does not test a hidden-product reveal or a strong delay of explicit product emphasis. The final hold provides genuine brand recovery but is visually flatter than the closed-eye sensory peak. The cut from the open smelling jar to a later closed jar also implies, rather than shows, the intervening close action.

This therefore advances the branded-recovery branch beyond the earlier unlabeled perfume proof, but it does not validate the complete `experience-before-product` Operator.

## Learned gate

Separate three claims: product presence during use, explicit product emphasis, and brand recovery. A readable label can complete brand recovery while simultaneously preventing a strong product-delay claim. Do not promote the whole Operator from one branch.

## Scope limit

This validates one locked late-night food ritual with a readable packaged brand. It does not validate hidden-product reveal, multi-angle advertising, product claims, social context, moving camera, music, or endorsement.
