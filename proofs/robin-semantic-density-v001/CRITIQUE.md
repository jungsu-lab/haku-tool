## Why this is not a pass

The single locked telephoto composition provides behavior change but no new distance, environment, body scale, relationship, or tactile information. With audio deliberately deferred, the long central hold is visually subtle and can read as merely waiting. It proves a limited animal-behavior branch of density weighting, not the stronger Haku structure in which a density hinge reveals materially new human, spatial, action, or tactile information.

The next proof must use coherent unedited coverage from one shooting world containing at least three distinct information roles, such as environment texture, action owner, embodied reaction, and a final tactile or relational destination.
