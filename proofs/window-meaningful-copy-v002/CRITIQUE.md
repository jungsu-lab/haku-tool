## What passes

The source supplies a complete real action and consequence: the subject contacts and opens the curtains, direct light enters, and her body settles inside the lit room. The plain and proof versions use identical frames, timing, crop, grade, and source motion.

Without copy, the result is a literal observation of a woman opening curtains. With “morning waited outside” in the dark state and “she let it in” after the light arrives, the action becomes a restrained proposition about inviting the day into the room. The second clause does not arrive before its visual evidence.

The revised lowercase regular sans text and one-pixel low-opacity shadow remain legible without overpowering curtain texture, body silhouette, or the natural light transition. No box, decorative line, red accent, animated type, freeze, retime, reverse, animated crop, animated scale, or padding was used.

## Limits

This proves meaningful copy in a solitary interior light-change situation. It does not prove dialogue, brand claims, group action, Korean typography, music synchronization, or copy over dense montage.

Audio remains deferred by the user. Research approval is not user acceptance.

## Learned gate

Copy must pass both a semantic gate and a visual hierarchy gate. It should appear only when the named state is visible, and its typography must remain quieter than the action that supplies the evidence.
