# Latte motif-state v001 critique

## Research verdict

`pass`

## What the source proves

- It is one fixed overhead take, not an edited stock montage.
- The same cup persists through dark base, first white contrast, swirl, recognizable
  pattern, refined rings, and completed surface.
- The coffee pattern advances monotonically and never resets to an earlier state.

## Plain versus proof

- Both variants contain 168 frames at 24 fps and use the same six source anchors.
- Plain assigns 28 frames to every state.
- Proof shortens the two setup states to 20 frames and assigns 46 frames to the
  completed motif.
- The 18-frame emphasis transfer makes the result state a destination rather than
  one more equally weighted sample.

## Boundary findings

- All five boundaries retain the cup, camera scale, and floor geometry.
- Large source-time skips read as material transformation because the spatial
  anchor stays fixed.
- Pitcher position changes, but each coffee surface is visibly more developed;
  no cut produces a backward reset.

## Scope

This passes the reusable `motif-state-return` grammar outside nature and
relationship footage. It does not by itself reproduce the full Haku mood, color,
or emotional world.
