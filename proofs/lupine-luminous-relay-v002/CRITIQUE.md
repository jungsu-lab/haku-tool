## What passes

The proof uses one coherent cottonbro lupine-field session and gives the same visible phenomenon four different carriers:

1. crisp flower shadow written on white fabric;
2. the shadow migrating onto the woman's hand;
3. that hand physically meeting the purple flower;
4. a purple foreground flower crossing and partially covering the man's face.

The plain and proof contain the same four materials, total duration, fixed crop, grade, and source-only motion. Reordering them into carrier progression changes the result from a coherent beauty montage into one accumulating tactile sensation. The three cuts preserve a visible property instead of relying on color similarity alone.

No synthetic flare, light overlay, exposure pump, freeze, reverse, retime, animated crop, animated scale, or last-frame padding is used.

## Limits

This does not validate every form of `luminous-texture-relay`. It validates real direct sunlight, flower shadow, and foreground floral occlusion in one session. Water reflection, glass refraction, lens flare, night light, and bridges between different locations still need independent proofs.

The result supports an atmospheric or relationship film. It does not prove product use, a brand claim, or advertising recovery.
