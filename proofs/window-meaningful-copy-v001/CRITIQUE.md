## What remains valid

The licensed unedited source provides one clean action spine: curtain contact, opening, light arrival, lit release. All source ranges are chronological and contiguous. No freeze, retime, reverse, animated crop, animated scale, or repeated-frame padding was used.

The two clauses are also evidence-bound. “Morning waited outside” belongs to the still-dark curtain state, while “she let it in” follows the visible arrival of light.

## Why this version fails

The all-caps bold serif and heavy default two-pixel shadow dominate a quiet image. At full resolution the type reads as generic promotional titling rather than restrained emotional punctuation. The copy may be semantically relevant, but its visual voice competes with the curtain texture and body silhouette.

This is not a source-selection or continuity failure. It is a typography hierarchy failure.

## Learned gate

Readable and meaningful copy can still fail when its weight, case, or shadow becomes the strongest object in the frame. The copy treatment must remain subordinate to the visible action that proves its proposition.
