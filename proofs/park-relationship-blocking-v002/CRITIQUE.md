## What passes

The proof progresses monotonically through six relationship states:

1. hand contact identifies the pair;
2. their bodies open into two distinct positions;
3. they reconverge;
4. the lift transfers action ownership;
5. carry and shared running turn the pair into one direction of travel;
6. the real empty path inherits their exit.

The plain and proof use the exact same six source ranges, fixed crop, grade, durations, and source-only motion. The plain's highlight-first order resets from lift to initial contact and later from post-run intimacy to earlier separation. The proof removes those relationship-state reversals.

The decisive hinge from reconvergence through lift, carry, running, and exit is preserved nearly continuously. Boundaries three and four join adjacent source time, so there is no pose skip or blocking lurch. The final boundary begins after both performers clear frame and holds live park motion.

There is no freeze, reverse, retime, repeated-frame padding, animated crop, animated scale, automatic zoom, or synthetic overlay.

## What v001 taught

Chronological labels alone do not create continuity. `v001` skipped the onset of the lift, jumped from a lifted pose to front-facing running, and mislabeled a close body crossing as empty space. Preserving the physical hinge mattered more than keeping the proof short.

## Limits

This validates one playful, camera-followed couple or friendship situation. It does not validate conflict, reconciliation, family hierarchy, locked-off staging, native vertical coverage, product advertising, or music synchronization.

The first two cuts are intentional state compression rather than seamless action matches. They pass because identity, park axis, touch, and relationship direction remain legible.
