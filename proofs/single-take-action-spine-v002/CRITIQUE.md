## Why the proof is stronger than the uniform plain

- The proof spends less time on the neutral and reach-apex states.
- It gives more time to the return and flower rise, so the gesture has consequence.
- It preserves a readable direction: empty reach → return → flowers.
- The climax is created by duration and action selection, not by an added effect.

## Learned editing rule

`state cuts must preserve either motion direction or pose geometry`

For long-take compression:

- identify preparation, execution, apex, return, consequence, and after-state;
- cut during motion only when the next state continues the same direction;
- otherwise cut on a pose match or a completed action;
- never use a later source range that resets the object backward merely because its still frame looks attractive;
- weight consequence and after-state longer than setup when the emotional destination matters.
