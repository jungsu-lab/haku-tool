## What works

- The operator generalizes from human relationship roles to environment, flow, texture, and living presence.
- The progressive panel reveal remains readable and does not look like decorative stickers.
- The proof has a clear sparse → medium → dense → breath → release curve.
- The robin crop correction is a true material-fit fix; no additional effect was needed.
- The ending has substantially more motion and visual scale than the preceding breath.

## Why this is only partial

- The sources are from unrelated shoots.
- Green forest/stream footage and the warm robin are compatible enough, but the saturated orange horizon announces a new stock source.
- The sequence communicates “nature and living motion” but not a specific remembered place, relationship, or event.
- The result risks reading as a curated nature montage rather than an authored Haku film.

## Learned gate

`motif continuity is not session continuity`

Repeated light, wind, water, and birds can support a poetic sequence, but they do not automatically erase differences in lens, color temperature, geography, or capture style. When those differences remain obvious:

- mark material fit `conditional`;
- do not add more overlays to hide the mismatch;
- prefer a same-location release shot or request two bridging shots;
- keep the proof in research status even if the technical render passes.

## Minimum material improvement

- Replace the orange sunset release with a same-forest or same-water wide shot containing moving birds, leaves, or a visible light change.
- Alternatively, add one real bridge from green-gold water reflection to orange horizon; do not synthesize the bridge with zoom or a flash transition.
