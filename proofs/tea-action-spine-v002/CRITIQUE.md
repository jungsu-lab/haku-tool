## What passes

The proof preserves the action owner and its physical hinge. The pot enters, the first stream forms, the complete pour body continues, and the pot releases from source 1.3 through 8.97 seconds in three source-contiguous ranges. The earlier version's disappearing-pot lurch is gone.

The only large source-time compression occurs after the pot has fully exited and before the later service hand begins. Because the cup, table, window, and background are locked and nothing changes during that omitted hold, the cut reads as removal of inactive time rather than a spatial reset.

The final hand action is allowed to complete the service detail instead of ending on the pour alone. The proof spends less time on untouched setup and more on the causal pour and consequence. No freeze, retime, reverse, zoom, animated crop, transition, or repeated-frame padding is used.

## Limits

The opaque floral cup hides its liquid level. The cup is a persistent anchor, but this source does not prove a fully visible before/after fill state. `motif-state-return` therefore remains a partial supporting observation and is not independently passed.

This validates a locked object ritual. It does not validate handheld action, multiple camera angles, branded product recovery, people-to-people interaction, or music synchronization.

Audio remains deferred by the user. Research approval is not user acceptance.

## Learned gate

Compress inactivity, not the object that owns the action. If a pour, lift, embrace, exit, or handoff supplies the causal hinge, preserve that hinge near-contiguously even when the edit must run longer.
