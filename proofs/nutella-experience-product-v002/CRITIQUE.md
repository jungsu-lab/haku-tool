## Improvement over V1

V1 ended with a 40-frame green-yellow product hold after a blue-violet closed-eye smell response. The hue break and long passive duration made the recovery feel flatter than the sensory peak.

V2 selects a 24-frame blue-violet hold from the same second take and gives six frames back to the smell response. The final cut now preserves the established television-light world, keeps the label readable, and ends before the passive pose dominates. The action, crop, label scale, and face position remain stable; no digital motion hides the repair.

## What passes

The proof preserves a complete real lid-opening spine, a near-contiguous lid-release-to-smell hinge, and a concise later brand recovery. The plain version still opens on the later closed-jar state and resets to the earlier opening action, so sequence order has a visible causal consequence.

## Why the Operator remains partial

The Nutella name is readable throughout the proof. This material therefore cannot test a hidden-product reveal or strong product delay. The later closed jar still implies an unshown closing action, and the recovery contributes no supported claim, packshot detail, or new product information beyond the readable brand.

## Learned gate

A product recovery should inherit the sensory peak's light world and occupy only the time needed for recognition. Extending a passive label hold beyond that point weakens, rather than strengthens, the advertising close.

## Scope limit

This improves one locked late-night branded food-smell ritual. Hidden reveal, explicit claims, multi-angle product coverage, social proof, camera movement, music, and endorsement remain untested.
