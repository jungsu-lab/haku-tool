## What is valid

The source contains a real stable blue–white–orange–red refracted band. The empty wall, fingertips, palm and turning hand visibly receive the same band at the same height. No artificial glow, generated light, exposure pumping, zoom, freeze, retime, reverse, or transition is used.

The first three boundaries preserve the optical carrier clearly enough to prove that this is not merely shared color.

## Why this version fails

The final two-hand to open-palm transition crosses an excessive near-camera hand enlargement. A dark silhouette abruptly covers the band and becomes the strongest visual object. This produces a scale lurch and obscures the optical destination.

The optical hypothesis is supported, but the finished edit is not approved while its final movement feels accidental.

## Learned gate

A real luminous carrier does not excuse a body- or object-scale lurch. The destination frame must keep the light phenomenon readable and cannot replace it with an unrelated dominant silhouette.
