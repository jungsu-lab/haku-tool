## Why it is only a partial pass

- The difference between uniform plain timing and proof timing is too small to make authorship obvious.
- The candle occupies little screen area.
- The smoke trace is almost invisible, so the ending can read as an ordinary fade-to-black.
- Two state cuts contain a small flame-orientation discontinuity. They are not technical glitches, but they weaken the sense of one continuous extinction.
- This proves a narrow material-state case, not the stronger person-to-empty-space version of the Operator.

## Learned gate

`absence needs a visible inherited trace`

Use `presence-to-absence-hold` only when the final state inherits at least one readable property from the established presence:

- the same place or surface;
- residual motion such as smoke, water, fabric, hair, dust, or light;
- an object displacement or bodily consequence;
- a stable composition that makes the missing subject legible.

If the trace is too small or disappears into pure black, request stronger coverage or choose a simpler ending. Do not add unrelated smoke, automatic zoom, a freeze, or exposure pumping to manufacture an afterimage.
