## What passes

The edit begins with live hand contact, so the composite has an action cause rather than appearing as a decorative template.

The three panels have distinct roles:

1. the dominant upper panel establishes shared presence;
2. the lower-left flower-framed exchange supplies intimate and tactile detail;
3. the lower-right empty park supplies place and an eventual afterimage.

They enter progressively at 0.6-second intervals. The field then releases to one full-frame reconvergence shot and a longer lift onset, restoring body-scale information and reducing density. All six plain/proof materials and the total duration are identical; the proof's difference is in-frame simultaneity, hierarchy, progressive entry, and release.

No border, drop shadow, typography, automatic zoom, freeze, reverse, retime, animated crop, repeated-frame padding, or unrelated overlay is used.

## Limits

The dominant source is shoulder-close, so it proves shared presence more strongly than full walking-body motion. The Operator record must not overstate it as a legible walking master.

This validates one same-session urban pair. Unrelated footage, product grids, native vertical layouts, typography, and music synchronization remain untested.

## Learned gate

A panel earns space by supplying a named information role that another panel cannot replace. Progressive entry is useful only when the completed field later releases to one stronger live image; otherwise the result remains a collage template.
